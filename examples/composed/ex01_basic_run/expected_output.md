## Expected Output – ex01

### Qualitative behavior

- Particles originate from a fixed point.
- Particles propagate along a fixed direction.
- Energy is deposited in a single sensitive volume.
- All recorded energy deposits belong to the same volume.

### Observable consequences

- The output contains energy deposition data.
- Only one volume name appears in the scoring output.
- Energy values are consistent with the selected particle and energy.

### Diagnostic interpretation

- No energy recorded → geometry, source, or scoring is incorrect.
- Multiple volume names → scoring configuration is incorrect.
