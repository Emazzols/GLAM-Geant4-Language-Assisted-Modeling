# Expected Output – ex04_Pinocchio

## Qualitative behavior

- The geometry forms a complex asymmetric structure.
- The hat is positioned above the head
  and oriented upward due to rotation.
- No overlap warnings occur if distances are correct.
- Electrons are emitted from a finite spherical region.

## Observable output

- All energy deposits are associated with the head volume.
- Energy deposition is localized due to short electron range.
- Particle tracks are influenced by nearby materials.

## Diagnostic checks

- Overlap warnings indicate incorrect placement distances.
- A misplaced hat indicates wrong offset or rotation.
- Energy recorded outside the head indicates scoring errors.
- Treating the source as point-like would alter the behavior.
