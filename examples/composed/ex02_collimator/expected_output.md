# Expected Output – ex02_stack_disk

## Qualitative behavior

- Particles are emitted from an extended disk source.
- A fraction of the particles is absorbed by the collimators.
- Surviving particles propagate through the full stack.
- Energy is deposited only in the detector volume.

## Observable consequences

- The output contains energy deposition entries
  associated exclusively with the detector.
- The total recorded energy is reduced
  due to collimation effects.
- No energy deposition is recorded in collimator volumes.

## Diagnostic interpretation

If energy deposits appear in any collimator,
the scoring configuration is incorrect.

If no energy is recorded in the detector,
the geometry alignment or source configuration is wrong.
