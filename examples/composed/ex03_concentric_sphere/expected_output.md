# Expected Output – ex03_concentric_sphere

## Qualitative behavior

- Gammas are emitted isotropically from the center.
- Energy is deposited first in the inner core.
- Particles propagate outward into successive layers.
- Outer layers receive attenuated energy deposition.

## Observable output

- Energy deposits are recorded in:
  - core
  - layer1
  - layer2
- The total deposited energy decreases with radius.
- Angular distributions are uniform.

## Diagnostic checks

- Any overlap warnings indicate incorrect hierarchy.
- Missing energy in a layer indicates a scoring configuration error.
- Anisotropic patterns indicate incorrect source setup.
