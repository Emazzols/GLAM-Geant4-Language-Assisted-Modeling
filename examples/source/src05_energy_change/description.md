# exS05 – Energy change in a point-like directional source

## Purpose

This example demonstrates that particle energy is an independent
parameter of the source.

It isolates the effect of energy from all other source properties.

## What this example demonstrates

- Change of particle energy
- Monoenergetic emission at a different energy
- Unchanged source geometry and direction

## What this example does NOT demonstrate

- Extended sources
- Angular distributions
- Geometry definition
- Scoring configuration
- Energy distributions

No assumptions are made about geometry or scoring.
Both are intentionally left empty.

## Conceptual focus

Only one parameter changes: energy.

All other source properties remain identical to the baseline example.

This enforces the idea that changing energy does not imply changing
position, direction, or source type.

## Why this example exists

LLMs tend to implicitly couple energy changes
with modifications to geometry or direction.

This example explicitly teaches that energy
is a standalone source parameter.
