# Expected Output

The geometry contains three independent box volumes placed inside the
world volume.

Each box:
- has identical dimensions
- is made of silicon
- is positioned explicitly using a manual placement
- is located at a different position along the Z axis

The boxes are not logically related to each other and do not form a
composite or repeated structure.

In the visualization, three separate boxes should appear aligned along
the Z direction with clear spacing between them.

No assumptions are made about particle sources or scoring.
