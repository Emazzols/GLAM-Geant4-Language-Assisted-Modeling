# geo09_place_stacked

This example demonstrates a compact way to place multiple volumes inside
the same mother volume following a regular pattern.

Several identical box-shaped volumes are defined independently and then
arranged along a common axis with a fixed spacing between them.

The stacking mechanism automatically computes the placement positions
based on the chosen axis and spacing, producing a simple and regular
layout.

This example focuses exclusively on geometry construction and placement.
No assumptions are made about particle sources or scoring.
