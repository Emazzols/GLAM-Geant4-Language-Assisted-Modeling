# Expected Output

The geometry contains multiple box volumes arranged in a regular stack
inside the world volume.

All volumes:
- have identical dimensions
- are made of plastic scintillator material
- are aligned along a common axis
- are equally spaced from one another

The resulting layout is equivalent to a manual placement of each volume
at regular intervals, but expressed in a more compact and structured way.

In the visualization, the boxes should appear aligned along the chosen
axis with uniform spacing.

No assumptions are made about particle sources or scoring.
