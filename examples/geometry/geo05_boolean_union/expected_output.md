- The geometry contains a single composite volume in addition to the world.
- The composite volume is the union of two box-shaped solids.
- The base box is wider and shorter.
- The second box is taller, narrower, and shifted upward along the Z axis.
- The material of the final volume is silicon.

In visualization mode:

- A single stepped solid is visible.
- The taller box protrudes from the top of the base box.
- The shape is symmetric around the Z axis.
- No separate child volumes are visible.

No assumptions are made about particle source or scoring configuration.
